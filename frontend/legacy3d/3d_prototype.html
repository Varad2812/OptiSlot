How it works

A mock marshalling room: 3 cabinets → 4 CIOCs each (2 front, 2 rear) → 8 baseplates → 12 CHARMs = 1,152 modules. About 20% are lit by IO type; the data is seeded mock data.

  • Click to zoom down a level, Esc to go back
  • Hover to preview what you will open
  • Search a tag (2-3 opens a CIOC, 2-3-5-7 a CHARM)
  • Upload Dive streams data from the junction box into the baseplates
  • All CHARMs are one instanced mesh, so a single draw call
